Monitors: Hard real-time — bounded response; deadline misses unacceptable
Tablets: Handheld/mobile — power management and wireless support
Workstations: Desktop/time-sharing — interactive multi-user applications
Database: Clustered — failover and continuous service
MRI imaging: Multiprocessor — parallel CPU and I/O throughput
Partner hospitals: Distributed — secure communication across sites
Rejected: one general-purpose OS everywhere; it cannot guarantee hard deadlines on monitors
